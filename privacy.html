      Privacy

      Privacy policy.

      Short version: Open Notch collects nothing, and nothing leaves your Mac unless you set up a phone notice yourself.

      What we collect

      Nothing. Open Notch has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no update checks. It makes no network requests of its own, unless you turn on Live Claude limits (below).

      What the app reads on your Mac

      • What your agents report through the hooks you install, kept in ~/.notchy.
      • The agents’ own logs (Claude Code transcripts, Codex rollouts, Command Code transcripts) to know their state and estimate token usage.
      • The process list, to find running agents and the apps they live in, and, while the notch is open on the Agents tab, their CPU and memory use.
      • Claude Code’s config file and status line, for your plan type and its 5-hour and weekly limits: never your e-mail, ids or login token.
      • Claude Code’s login, only if you turn on Live Claude limits (Settings → Usage, off by default): its access token, read from your Keychain to ask Anthropic for fresh limits, and never stored.
      • Browser tab titles and addresses, only if you turn on tracking AI in browsers, and only tabs on the list of AI sites are kept.
      • Git’s HEAD file in an agent’s folder, for its branch and worktree.

      All of it stays on your Mac, in a folder only you can read. Secrets (tokens, keys, passwords, connection strings) are removed from anything shown.

      The one thing it can send

      If you turn on notices to your phone and give it a web address (ntfy, Slack, Discord, Telegram or your own), Open Notch posts a short notice there when an agent needs you or finishes. A notice says which agent and what it needs, and optionally the project name. It never carries a command, a path or file contents. Only https is used, and the address is stored in a file only you can read.

      This website

      This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of serving them.

      Removing everything

      notchyctl uninstall --yes removes Open Notch’s hooks, mod, links and its data folder. See Uninstall.

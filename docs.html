      Using the notch

      • The notchReading the compact notch, the open list, each row, sub-agents, worktrees, resource usage and the grid view.
      • Approvals and questionsAllow, deny or answer an agent from the notch, what Always allow remembers, and the safety rules that decide what can be approved.
      • Terminal and repliesA real terminal in the notch, and replying to an agent without switching to its window.
      • UsageThe Usage tab's limits and token estimates, the Usage pane in Settings, and adding your own prices for a cost estimate.
      • Media trayDrop screenshots, designs, PDFs and logs on the notch, and every agent can see them.
      • Alerts and phone noticesSounds for each kind of event, quiet hours, reminders, and short notices to your phone while you are away.
      • Keyboard shortcutsOpen the notch, approve, deny and jump to the agent that needs you without touching the mouse.
      • AccessibilityVoiceOver, the keyboard, contrast and Reduce Motion in Open Notch, what was checked, and what is still missing.
      • Phone remoteYour agents and their cards on your phone, over your own Wi-Fi. Allow, deny, say why and answer questions from the couch, with nothing going through the internet.

      Agents

      • Claude CodeHooks for exact status, approvals and questions answered from the notch, context and plan usage.

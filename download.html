      1. 1

        Drag it into Applications

        Open the disk image and drag Open Notch onto the Applications folder.

      2. 2

        Open it the first time

        Open Notch isn’t notarised by Apple yet, so macOS says it “can’t verify” it. Right-click it in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. Only once.
